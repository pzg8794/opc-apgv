\begin{normalsize}\normalfont \normalsize \textnormal{
This evaluation report is based on the overall performance and behavior of each member in the group, including me. As a group, we accomplished our goal and met the specifications set for our project. Nevertheless, we experienced situations that could have got out control if a smart approach was not taken at that exact time. In order to give a more clear and concise report, I have divided this group evaluation into Homework and Project sections.\newline}
\end{normalsize}

\section{Homework}
\label{homework}\normalfont \normalsize{
	All members had qualities about themselves that directly affected the group in positive and negative way. These, however, were modeled throughout the course for the benefits of our group.\newline}

	\subsection{Anshika}\normalfont \normalsize{
		Anshika, she is willing to get the work done and on-time one way or the other. This was one of the reason why I was willing to work with her from day one. On the other hand, she is somewhat careless with the work she does. As a result, we had to proofread her work and make sure it was proper, genuine, and right.\newline }


	\subsection{Wander}\normalfont \normalsize{
		Wander, he is a hands on person. Once you meet with him to do the homework, you can be certain it will be done and right way. Conversely, he is not organized when it comes to time management. He would text message all the sudden and ask you to come to work on the homework. It is hard for him to commit to a schedule. Nowadays, the right thing to do is to give your team at least two days in advance so they can reschedule and coordinate their time accordingly.\newline}

	\subsection{Piter}\normalfont \normalsize{
		In the beginning, we started solving the homework and comparing the answers. Then, after we saw the outcome of the first homework submission, I took over the homework submission. To avoid one person from doing most of the work, we decided to assign a question to each member. Once done, I would check, proofread and format our work for submission purpose. One thing I noticed about myself is that I am obsessed with trying to do things perfects. Sometimes, these things would make me spend a lot of time in small details instead of focusing in the big picture. \newline}
		
		\normalfont \normalsize{Overall, we discussed most of the homework and came up with solutions as a group. Sometimes, we tried learning from each other by allowing each one to solve questions on the board. We learned very much from this because it helped us understand and discuss different point of views.\newline}
		

\section{Project}
\label{project}\normalfont \normalsize{
	Many actions were taken by each team member during our project development. Some of them were appropriate, others were not. I must say, there were times in which I found myself in very difficult situations. Nonetheless, I must mention that using blogs for projects updates, including the interaction among members, helped me organized my thoughts and think more clearly. I must admit, bringing out the issues and differences among us helped somewhat to improve our communication as a group.\newline}

	\subsection{Anshika}\normalfont \normalsize{
		Anshika, she was able to get done the job assigned. As I mentioned before, she always manages to get the work done one way or the other. Nevertheless, she took longer than what we expected to format our database. This caused a delay in our group, forcing us to get the job done right on time.\newline }

	\subsection{Wander}\normalfont \normalsize{
		Wander, he is a very good troubleshooter and got the job done in an efficient manner. We were behind our goal because of the data formatting, however, Wander troubleshooting skills were very helpful and essential in our project to succeed in our overall goal. Though, Wander still has not learned how to work in group. He did a lot of work and search on his own without consulting the group, forcing the group to suddenly make changes.\newline }

	\subsection{Piter}\normalfont \normalsize{
		My main task was to help wander with the interface development. Most of the troubleshooting was done by Wander. It was difficult for me to troubleshoot issues we were having because I was helping both of them, $“$individually,$”$ to speed up the process. I helped Anshika with the formatting task because she was not able to get it started. At the same time, I had to make sure the work done in the homework and or project was done right, properly and professionally. I must admit, sometimes I felt overloaded because I was not able to work on anything else but this group. Despite, I still made myself available to schedule, discuss, troubleshoot, fix and implement multiple approaches until we got an answer or the work done. Another thing I noticed about myself is that I tend to take more than what I can handle. This, for the most part, does not allow me to make use of my knowledge and skills in a maximum scale.\newline}
		
		\normalfont \normalsize{The following is to me one of the most unprofessional behavior that happened within our group. Once I realized that Anshika was having more issues formatting a new set of data, which I personally struggled with. I suggested to work with the data we had since it was good enough for our purpose. The goal of our project was to implement big data and data mining techniques on a project of matter. They refused using the data because it was monthly and not daily, and according to them using the monthly data would be a lie. I approached the instructor to discuss the matter and see what we can do since we were running out of time. Despite of the fact I confirmed with the instructor, they still refused. This behavior, from my experience, was very stubborn and unprofessional and it would not have been allowed in any company.\newline}
		
		\normalfont \normalsize{I realized the work was delivered right, but we could have got it done earlier and make sure that it was perfect instead of rushing days before the deadline. Working with Anshika and Wander has taught me a lot about working in group. They both worked hard and tried their best to get the job done. I feel that my experience in the field, and working in group, helped us to come up with strategies and schedules to make sure that our group was getting some progress done every week.\newline }
